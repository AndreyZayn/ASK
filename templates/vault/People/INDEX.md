# People Index

Navigate by the relevant authorized scope before looking up a person:

- [[People/personal/INDEX]] — owner-authorized personal context

Use the selected scope registry to find an existing record before creating one. Check names, known aliases, and relevant context for ambiguity or duplicates; do not merge different people based only on a matching name. Resolve uncertain identity with the owner. A person record uses `kind: person` and the locally supported scope; record only necessary, owner-authorized context and its source. Do not infer relationships or store credentials.

Add a project-scoped registry only when authorized and useful, then link it here and from that project's navigation. Do not invent project scopes or copy personal context into project records without authority. Keep scope boundaries clear; link a confirmed existing record when appropriate instead of duplicating it blindly.

No people are supplied. Existing installations may retain a flat or custom People layout; follow their current indexes and conventions. This starter does not migrate, move, or overwrite their records.
