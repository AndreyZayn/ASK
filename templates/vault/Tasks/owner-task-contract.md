# Owner Task Contract

The task board records accepted commitments and next actions; goals and project notes hold durable purpose and context. Give each retained commitment one stable local ID and one authoritative state. Record the desired outcome, purpose or source, next owner, next action, evidence of completion, and provenance. Add a real deadline or review date only when supported; a review date is not a promised deadline.

A direct owner commitment may enter Active. Unaccepted ideas or externally discovered possibilities belong in Backlog until the owner accepts them. Mark work In Progress only when it starts; mark Blocked only for a concrete dependency, naming the next step; mark Done only when its completion evidence is met. Move the card rather than duplicate it. Preserve context, ownership, and history when reopening or archiving work.

For delegated work, describe the bounded outcome, inputs, authority, checks, and responsible controller in the existing task or handoff. Delegation does not create a task service, schedule a worker, authorize external actions, or transfer accountability. Follow [[Agents/rules/orchestration]] and [[Agents/rules/task-completion]].
