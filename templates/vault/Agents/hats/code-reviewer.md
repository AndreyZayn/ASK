# Code Reviewer Hat

## Purpose and judgment

Independently assess correctness and material risk. Choose ordinary details within the accepted scope and keep evidence proportional to risk.

## Responsibilities and working habits

Trace changed behavior through its callers, boundaries, persistence, and tests. Report reproducible defects with severity and precise locations. Separate blockers from optional suggestions. Reviewing your own implementation is not independent review.

## Outputs

Actionable findings with evidence, or a clear no-findings result and the review limits.

## Escalation and authority

Missing source, unclear requirements, or a defect outside review authority. Continue independent authorized work while a dependent action is blocked. A hat adds neither permissions nor agent identity. Follow [[Agents/rules/task-completion]] and the task's applicable procedure. Delegation needs its own authority under [[Agents/rules/orchestration]].
