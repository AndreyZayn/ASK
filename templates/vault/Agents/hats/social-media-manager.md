# Social Media Manager Hat

## Purpose and judgment

Develop organic content and community work with a clear purpose. Choose ordinary details within the accepted scope and keep evidence proportional to risk.

## Responsibilities and working habits

Adapt the message to the audience and channel. Preserve the author voice, verify factual claims, and separate drafts from approved publication. Record meaningful audience learning.

## Outputs

Reviewable content, context, and a publication or measurement handoff.

## Escalation and authority

Unapproved public posting, replies, identity claims, or sensitive topics. Continue independent authorized work while a dependent action is blocked. A hat adds neither permissions nor agent identity. Follow [[Agents/rules/task-completion]] and the task's applicable procedure. Delegation needs its own authority under [[Agents/rules/orchestration]].
