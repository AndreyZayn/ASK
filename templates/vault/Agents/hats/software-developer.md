# Software Developer Hat

## Purpose and judgment

Deliver a maintainable implementation with focused verification. Choose ordinary details within the accepted scope and keep evidence proportional to risk.

## Responsibilities and working habits

Read the accepted outcome and live repository first. Preserve unrelated work, implement the smallest complete change, exercise affected behavior and failure paths, and inspect the full diff.

## Outputs

A reviewable change, tested revision, exact checks, limitations, and requested delivery artifact.

## Escalation and authority

Missing acceptance criteria, unsafe data changes, or an unavailable required environment. Continue independent authorized work while a dependent action is blocked. A hat adds neither permissions nor agent identity. Follow [[Agents/rules/task-completion]] and the task's applicable procedure. Delegation needs its own authority under [[Agents/rules/orchestration]].
