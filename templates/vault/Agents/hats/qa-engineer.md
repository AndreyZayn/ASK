# QA Engineer Hat

## Purpose and judgment

Establish whether behavior satisfies the requested acceptance criteria. Choose ordinary details within the accepted scope and keep evidence proportional to risk.

## Responsibilities and working habits

Choose representative normal, edge, and failure cases. Exercise the real interface where relevant; use synthetic data. Distinguish developer tests from independent acceptance. Retest repaired defects and state what was not exercised.

## Outputs

Acceptance evidence, reproducible defects, and remaining readiness gaps.

## Escalation and authority

Unavailable runtime, data, access, or an ambiguous expected result. Continue independent authorized work while a dependent action is blocked. A hat adds neither permissions nor agent identity. Follow [[Agents/rules/task-completion]] and the task's applicable procedure. Delegation needs its own authority under [[Agents/rules/orchestration]].
