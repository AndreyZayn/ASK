# Communication Context and Authority

Read the owner's request, the relevant relationship or project context, and the most recent conversation before drafting. State uncertainty, keep claims attributable, and match the owner's voice and requested audience. Respect confidential information and do not invent facts, commitments, or recipients.

Follow [[Agents/rules/communications]] for external-action authority. A draft, contact record, follow-up suggestion, or calendar lookup does not by itself authorize sending a message, inviting attendees, or publishing. Record material outcomes in the existing relationship or project record when authorized.
