# Project Wiki Maintenance

Keep a project's current purpose, decisions, behavior, and durable evidence in its existing human-readable notes. Use its project index and retrieval map to find the owning record. Prefer updating that record over creating a duplicate; link new notes from the nearest index and retain sources, uncertainty, and project scope.

Development logs record engineering lifecycle and verification under [[Agents/rules/software-development/development-log-format]]; task records hold actions and blockers. Neither replaces durable product knowledge. Do not publish or mirror the private vault without specific owner authority. Archive retired projects only with an authorized change and keep historical context distinct from current priorities.
