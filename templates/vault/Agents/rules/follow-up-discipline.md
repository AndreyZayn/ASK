# Follow-up Discipline

Use the owner's instructions, the actual conversation, explicit commitments, and any stated deadline to decide whether a follow-up is useful. Distinguish unanswered outreach, a promised action, an incoming request, and relationship maintenance. Record the evidence, next owner, and next meaningful action in the existing task or relationship record; do not invent a commitment or assume a universal cadence.

Prepare a relevant draft and surface time-sensitive work to the owner through an authorized channel. Respect a person's preferences and stop when asked. Capturing a contact or suggesting a follow-up does not authorize sending a message; follow [[Agents/rules/communications]] and the owner's instructions.
