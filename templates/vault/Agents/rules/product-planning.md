# Product Planning

For new user-facing scope, establish the intended user, problem, context, outcome, evidence, and material uncertainties before defining features or acceptance criteria. Use existing project research and decisions before inventing a new persona or duplicating a plan. Separate observed facts from assumptions and open choices; choose the smallest useful next evidence.

Record durable product decisions in the owning project note and trace implementation work to the relevant requirements when the project uses such a system. Exploration and planning do not authorize implementation or publication. Technical repairs, dependency updates, and refactors follow the applicable software procedure without inventing product scope.
