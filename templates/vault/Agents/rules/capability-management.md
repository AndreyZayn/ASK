# Capability Management Rules

Catalogs in [[Agents/capabilities/INDEX]] describe what has been reviewed, not what is installed or authorized. Ordinary descriptive entries may be updated within authorized content access; that does not grant a capability. Installation, upgrade, connection, authentication, scope expansion, and changes to runtime settings require owner direction. Inspect provenance and permissions before recommending a capability. Keep credentials outside the vault and Git; never copy third-party or proprietary skill text into public notes.
