# Skill Management

The runtime's available skills and its actual configuration are operational evidence. A catalog describes a point-in-time observation; it does not install, enable, or grant an agent permission to use a skill. During onboarding record only evidence the current runtime exposes, its scope and limitations, and whether discovery was complete. Do not infer absent or inaccessible capabilities.

Keep a lightweight observed inventory distinct from curated records for skills the owner deliberately adopts or manages. For a curated record describe its purpose, approved users or runtimes, source and verification, and maintenance responsibility without copying credentials or private content. Creating, installing, modifying, assigning, or retiring a skill requires the owner's applicable instruction and runtime access. Follow [[Agents/rules/capability-management]] and [[knowledge-continuity-loop]].
