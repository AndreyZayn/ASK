# Automation Management

An automation is a deployed trigger, actor, payload, and delivery configuration; a note or loop does not start a job. Before creating, changing, pausing, or removing one, establish the owner's authority, intended task, trigger and timezone, runtime, access, delivery target, and stopping conditions. Preserve unrelated assignments and credentials. Document the authored payload and its scope in the owner's automation records.

After an authorized change, read back the actual deployment and test or observe its first applicable run. Separate configuration, successful delivery, and useful task completion; report untested behavior and precise failures. Reconcile previous runs and side effects before retrying. Do not expand permissions, send messages, or schedule recurring work merely because a loop exists.
