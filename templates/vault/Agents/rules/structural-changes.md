# Structural Change Rules

Routine updates and new indexed notes within authorized paths do not change the framework; root [[../../INDEX]] and [[../../date]] are routine editable notes, not protected just because they bootstrap agents. Adding or moving areas, changing bootstrap order, [[agents]], [[knowledge-map]], [[knowledge-continuity-loop]], rules, the schema or tag taxonomy, roster, ontology, access declarations, or actual runtime permissions requires explicit owner direction and a reviewed migration path. Compare template and schema diffs before applying updates; do not silently rewrite customized notes. Validate links and keep [[knowledge-map]] and [[../../INDEX]] consistent after an approved change.
