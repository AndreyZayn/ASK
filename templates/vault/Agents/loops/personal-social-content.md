# Personal Social Content Loop

Begin only with an authorized request, audience, channel, relevant sources, and an agreed outcome. Use the owner's established voice and evidence; distinguish original views from sourced facts. Draft, review for accuracy and confidential material, and refine with the owner's feedback. Preserve useful decisions in the appropriate existing record.

Drafting does not authorize publication or external communication. Follow [[Agents/rules/communication-rules]], observe the requested stopping point, and report what remains unverified. A loop definition neither schedules nor posts content.
